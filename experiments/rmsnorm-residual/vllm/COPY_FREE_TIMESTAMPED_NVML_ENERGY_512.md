# Qualify timestamped NVML energy samples on H100

Serializing the NVML calls yielded two complete independent fixed-work seeds with about 2.24% lower board energy per row for four warps. The third seed missed its required second order after opening or closing calls took more than 50 ms. Ascertain whether each counter value's reported sample time gives a tighter record of the work interval. [Short-boundary result](ENERGY_COUNTER_BOUNDARIES_512_REPORT.md) · [Tracking issue](https://github.com/hyscodebase/h100-rmsnorm-lab/issues/54)

On seed 201, match the original BF16 outputs and input and weight digests, and load the same audited kernels. Query `nvmlDeviceGetFieldValues` for `NVML_FI_DEV_TOTAL_ENERGY_CONSUMPTION` (field 83). Save the API and per-field status, value type, raw cumulative millijoules, field timestamp and update latency. Archive failures and check that successful field values and sample times advance through a fixed-work probe. [NVML field queries](https://docs.nvidia.com/deploy/archive/R550/nvml-api/group__nvmlFieldValueQueries.html) · [Field definitions](https://docs.nvidia.com/deploy/archive/R550/nvml-api/group__nvmlFieldValueEnums.html)

Record pairs of monotonic readings around realtime reads near each NVML query. Use those pairs to map the reported CPU epoch timestamp to a conservative monotonic interval. An energy window qualifies when the two sample intervals bracket all completed work and the greatest combined padding allowed by their endpoints is at most 50 ms. Preserve the API's query brackets independently. Reject clock mappings or field statuses that leave the sample bounds unresolved. [NVML timestamp definition](https://docs.nvidia.com/deploy/archive/R550/nvml-api/structnvmlFieldValue__t.html)

If seed 201 qualifies, use independent seeds 202–204 one at a time. Keep 512 bursts per period, three seconds of warm-up, both opposite backend orders and the one-repeat cap for the first rejected pair. Require three exact seeds with 1.5% lower median energy per row and at most 0.5% lower throughput. Record field times, identities, raw work and energy, paired decisions, auxiliary samples, attempts and one cache-only recovery.
