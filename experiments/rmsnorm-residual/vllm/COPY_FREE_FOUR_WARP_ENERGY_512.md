# Compare device energy for the same residual RMSNorm work

At 512 × 4,096 the four-warp kernel matched both BF16 outputs and gave up 0.032–0.090% throughput in three independent seeds. Its reported board power medians were 10.07–11.56 W lower. Test whether it consumes less board energy for the same number of completed rows. [Prior throughput and power samples](FOUR_PHYSICAL_WARPS_512_REPORT.md) · [Tracking issue](https://github.com/hyscodebase/h100-rmsnorm-lab/issues/50)

Keep the two audited binaries, released vLLM oracle, eight CUDA streams, 32 disjoint pairs per stream and original BF16 rounding and writable buffer contract. Seed 193 checks eight numerical fixtures, both replayed outputs, alias guards and the original input and weight digests. Use the assigned CUDA device's NVML cumulative energy counter. Verify that reads succeed and that the counter advances over active work before admitting independent samples. Preserve an unsupported-counter result if the server cannot report it. [NVML device queries](https://docs.nvidia.com/deploy/archive/R550/nvml-api/group__nvmlDeviceQueries.html)

Measure seeds 194–196 independently, one at a time. Run two balanced periods per backend in each seed. Each period follows at least three seconds of warm-up and executes exactly 512 measured bursts of 32 graph replays per stream. Each period completes 2,147,483,648 rows. Record cumulative millijoules at both synchronized boundaries, query timestamps and elapsed wall/CUDA timing. Compute joules per billion rows from the counter delta and the completed rows. The device-wide delta includes the gaps between replays within the measured interval. Warm-up and output verification occur outside the timed boundaries.

Both outputs and the source hashes must still match after timing. Require a median of at least 1.5% less energy per row and at most 0.5% lower wall throughput in every independent seed. Archive raw counter values, queries, completed work and intervals, direct and supplemental telemetry, kernel identities, attempts and cache-only recovery.
